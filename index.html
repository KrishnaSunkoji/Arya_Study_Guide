How to use this guide

Priority system (same across all subjects)

  • MUST DO — in every or almost every paper. Do first, no exceptions.
  • HIGH — in 4–5 of 6 papers. Your safety buffer.
  • MEDIUM — in 2–4 papers. Add these after MUST + HIGH are solid.
  • LOW — rare or never asked as a full question. MCQ-level only.

Scoring the 65+

  • 14 MCQ marks are the cheapest marks in the paper — target 14/14 in every subject.
  • You can drop ~5 marks in the 56-mark written section and still hit 65+.
  • Choice is built into Q2–Q5 (attempt any 4 of 5 / any 2 of 3) — you never need every topic.
  • Complete, well-structured answers beat partially-done safe answers. Half an answer loses more than a weak topic avoids.

Exam-day approach (all subjects)

  • Spend the first 30 min on all 14 MCQs. Don't leave any blank.
  • Before picking written questions, read all options — choose completeness over perceived difficulty.
  • 4-mark answers: 1 diagram + 4–5 points ≈ 10 min.
  • 6-mark answers: full derivation or diagram + steps + boxed answer ≈ 15 min.
  • Don't start a new topic 24 hrs before the exam.

Study roadmap — exactly this order
STEP 1
Foundations — Units 1 & 2
OL vs CL + feedback effects + sensitivity (quick theory) → F-V and F-I analogy (memorise mapping table cold) → RLC network T.F. (practise 3–4 numericals) → Mechanical system equations → T.F. → AC/DC servomotor derivation (learn once).
Why first: Units 3–6 assume you can write Laplace-domain equations.
STEP 2
Block Diagrams, SFG & Mason's — Unit 3 · highest-value unit
Block diagram reduction rules (series, parallel, feedback, moving points) → SFG terminology → Mason's Gain Formula (practise on 5–6 SFGs until automatic) → Combined: block diagram → SFG → Mason's in one question.
~17 marks · appears as 2 separate questions in almost every paper.
STEP 3
Time Response — Unit 4
2nd-order numericals (ζ, ωn, Mp, tp, ts, tr from given G(s)) → Derive rise time & peak time for underdamped system → Steady-state error + Kp/Kv/Ka for type-0/1/2 systems → Controllers P, PI, PD, PID (what each does, diagrams).
~16 marks · uses T.F. skill from Step 1.
STEP 4
Stability & Root Locus — Unit 5
Routh-Hurwitz + special cases (row of zeros, first-column zero) — practise 4–5 equations → Pole location in s-plane vs stability → Root locus: asymptotes, breakaway, angle of departure → Sketch 3-pole systems and comment on stability.
~14 marks · stability concepts reused in Bode (Step 5).
STEP 5
Frequency Response — Unit 6
Frequency domain specs (GM, PM, BW, resonant peak — definitions) → Bode plot: one fixed 5-step method (magnitude + phase, find ωgc & ωpc, read GM & PM, comment on stability) — repeat on 4–5 past-paper G(s)H(s) examples.
~10 marks · always 6 marks for the Bode question — nearly guaranteed once method is memorised.
FINAL REVISION
3–4 days before exam
Day −3: Redo Top 20 from memory, no notes, timed. Day −2: MCQ checklist (15 min) + re-derive 5 MUST derivations by hand. Day −1: Solve Apr 2026 paper timed. Morning of exam: only re-read MCQ checklist + F-V/F-I table + Bode procedure steps. Don't learn anything new in the final 24 hours.

14/14 MCQ checklist — know these cold before the exam
Transient responseDue to stored energy in system components.
Feedback effect↑ stability, ↑ bandwidth, ↓ sensitivity, ↓ disturbance effect.
F-V analogyMass→L, Spring→1/C, Friction→R, Force→V, Velocity→current.
F-I analogyMass→C, Spring→1/L, Friction→1/R, Force→current, Velocity→V.
Best test signalUnit impulse (contains all frequencies).
Type-N systemN poles at origin. Type-0: Kp finite. Type-1: Kp=∞, Kv finite. Type-2: Kp=Kv=∞, Ka finite.
AC Servomotor2-phase induction motor. Synchro = electromagnetic transducer.
Stability condition (Bode)GM > 0 dB AND PM > 0° — both must be positive simultaneously.
Root locus basicsBranches = no. of open-loop poles; symmetric about the real axis.
Root locus angle condition±(2q+1)×180°. Increasing K can reduce damping ratio.
Routh-HurwitzSign change in first column → roots in RHP → unstable.
Bode slopesGain K → 0 dB/dec; simple pole → −20 dB/dec; simple zero → +20 dB/dec.
Decade vs OctaveDecade: ω₂/ω₁ = 10. Octave: ω₂/ω₁ = 2.
Error coefficientsKp = lim(s→0) G(s); Kv = lim(s→0) sG(s); Ka = lim(s→0) s²G(s).
Common trapRise time is TIME-domain, NOT frequency-domain. "Which is NOT a freq-domain spec" → rise time.

Study stages (do in order)
STAGE 1 — ~4 hrs
Unit 1: Vectors & coordinate systems
Dot/cross product, unit vector, component of E along F, perpendicular unit vector → Gradient, divergence, curl + prove ∇·(∇×V)=0 → Cylindrical & spherical: conversion formulas + dv → Transform a vector at a point (3 examples each system).
Move on when: transform A=4ax−2ay−4az at (2,3,5) to cylindrical in 5 min without notes. Know all three dv expressions from memory.
STAGE 2 — ~10–12 hrs
Unit 2: Coulomb's law and Gauss (biggest block)
Coulomb's law (vector form), E definition, force between charges (triangle problem) → E from point, line and sheet charge — derive infinite sheet → Gauss's law: proof + two applications → Divergence theorem: verify both sides, find charge from D → Potential: E=−∇V, work done → Derive Maxwell's 1st equation (∇·D=ρv) from Gauss right now — asked in 5/6 papers.
Move on when: derive Gauss + one application, derive E for infinite sheet from scratch, solve 75π and 32 C problems.
STAGE 3 — ~4–5 hrs
Unit 3: Conductors and dielectrics
Parallel-plate capacitor: derive C=εA/d + numeric (asked 4/6 papers) → J=σE, continuity equation ∇·J=−∂ρ/∂t → Poisson's and Laplace's equations (derive) + uniqueness theorem → Electrostatic energy density + electric boundary conditions (MCQ insurance).
Move on when: capacitor derivation, continuity equation, Poisson's equation once from memory.
STAGE 4 — ~12–14 hrs
Unit 4: Static magnetic field (can be 28 marks in one paper)
Biot–Savart → H of finite filament → infinite filament → circular loop on axis → Ampère's circuital law: proof, point form ∇×H=J, one application → Stokes' theorem: verify both sides on the y²z/x problem → Magnetic flux density (∇·B=0) + vector magnetic potential (B=∇×A) → Lorentz force, force on wire, force between current elements.
Move on when: all three filament-H numericals, prove Ampère's law, verify Stokes' without peeking.
STAGE 5 — ~6–7 hrs
Unit 5: Fields in magnetic materials
Magnetic boundary conditions — derive both (most repeated 6-marker of Section II) → Inductance of solenoid and toroid (rectangular cross-section); energy ½LI² and energy density ½μH² → Dia/para/ferro/anti-ferro classification → Potential energy and forces on a magnetic material.
Move on when: both boundary conditions and toroid inductance in under 12 minutes.
STAGE 6 — ~3–4 hrs
Unit 6: Maxwell's equations
All four equations in point and integral form + word statements (6-marker in Apr 2026) → Displacement current Jd=∂D/∂t; conduction vs displacement current in lossy dielectric → Time-varying and harmonic (jω) forms.
Move on when: write all four equations in both forms, name each, state each in words.
STAGE 7 — FINAL
Revision: no new topics
MCQ checklist twice → redo all practice numericals timed → sit Apr 2026 first, then Mar 2024 as two 3-hour papers (MCQs in first 30 min each) → fix only what you got wrong.

Unit vectorMagnitude = 1, with a direction. A·B=0 → perpendicular; A×B=0 → parallel.
Triangle area½|A×B|
OperatorsGradient: scalar→vector. Divergence: vector→scalar. Curl: vector→vector.

dv expressionsCylindrical: ρ dρ dφ dz. Spherical: r² sinθ dr dθ dφ.

Gauss's lawTotal flux through closed surface = charge enclosed.
Infinite sheetE=ρs/2ε. E=0 inside a hollow charged sphere.
CapacitanceC=Q/V. Unit: C/V = F. ↑ with area, ↓ with distance. C≈70.8 pF for the standard numeric.
Energy densityElectric: ½εE². Magnetic: ½μH² = ½BH.
Ampère's law∇×H=J and ∮H·dl=I are both Ampère's law.
Biot–SavartGives magnetic field of a current element. Analogue of Coulomb's law.
B and AB=∇×A (vector potential); ∇·B=0; ∇·H=0.
Lorentz forceF=Q(E+v×B). Wire: F=IL×B.

SC MVA (transformer)SC MVA = rating / X_pu. 500 kVA, 5% reactance → 10 MVA.
CB MVA formulaCB MVA = √3 × pre-fault kV × SC kA. (NOT post-fault.)
Fault current factorsDepends on total impedance up to fault AND voltage at fault point. Fault level = fault MVA.
Reactance orderX_leakage < X″ (sub-transient) < X′ (transient) < X_sync. DC offset = unidirectional component.

Sequence networksBalanced system → positive sequence only. No neutral → I₀=0. Emf in positive-sequence network only.
LG faultThree networks in series. Ia1=Ia2=Ia0. Ungrounded neutral → Z₀=∞ → fault current=0.
LL faultNo zero-sequence network. I₀=0. Ia1=−Ia2.
LLG faultZero and negative networks in parallel. Open conductor = series fault.
Y-bus factsSize: n×n. Off-diagonal = mutual/transfer admittance. Diagonal = self/driving-point admittance.

EACUsed for stability analysis. Assumptions: resistance neglected, constant speed, constant Pm → "all of these". Max power at δ=90°.
